ArtistHub Account Deletion

PrinStarc · Account and data deletion requests

Last updated: November 22, 2026

ArtistHub users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting our support email. This page is provided for users who no longer wish to use ArtistHub.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to prinstarc.official@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line ArtistHub Account Deletion Request.
  3. In the message, provide the email address associated with your ArtistHub account and your username (if available). Do not send your password.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Email us to request account deletion

What may be deleted

After we verify and process your request, we intend to delete or de-identify the account and personal data associated with it that we can delete, including account/profile information and personal data associated with your use of ArtistHub, subject to technical limitations and applicable legal requirements.

Content and retention

Content you posted, such as music, videos, comments, and other uploads, may be associated with your account. We will review deletion requests for associated content as part of processing them. Certain information may be retained where required by law, needed for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate recordkeeping. If any data is retained, it will be limited to the applicable purpose and period.

Questions

For account deletion or privacy questions, contact prinstarc.official@gmail.com.
