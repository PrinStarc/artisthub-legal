ArtistHub Privacy Policy

Privacy matters to us. Here's how ArtistHub handles information.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ArtistHub, developed by PrinStarc (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the ArtistHub mobile application and related services (“ArtistHub” or the “Service”). Please read it before using the Service.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, ArtistHub may handle:

  • Account and profile information: information used to create or authenticate your account, such as email address, display name, username, and profile details.
  • User content: music/audio, short videos, images, captions, comments, and other content you upload or share.
  • Interactions: likes, comments, follows, shares, searches, and other activity associated with features you use.
  • Technical information: device, app, and diagnostic information that may be provided by the app, operating system, or service providers to operate, maintain, and protect the Service.
  • Support communications: information you send us when you contact support or report a concern.

2. How We Use Information

We use information, as applicable, to create and maintain accounts; authenticate users; provide profiles, music, video, search, and community features; display and manage content; process likes, comments, and other interactions; provide notifications you enable; respond to support requests; maintain security; troubleshoot problems; and improve the Service.

3. Service Providers

ArtistHub uses Firebase Authentication for supported sign-in and authentication services. ArtistHub uses Supabase for backend services and app data. These providers process information as needed to provide their services to ArtistHub. Their processing is also subject to their own terms and privacy documentation.

4. Content You Share

Content you choose to post—such as music, videos, profile information, and comments—may be visible to other users according to the features and settings of ArtistHub. Do not post information you do not want other users to see. You are responsible for ensuring you have the rights and permissions needed to share content.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers described above, made visible to other users through the Service's social and content features, or disclosed where reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce our rules, comply with law, or respond to lawful requests.

6. Notifications

If you enable notifications, ArtistHub may send notifications about app activity and features. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

7. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, operate accounts and features, maintain security, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies. You may request account and associated personal-data deletion by emailing prinstarc.official@gmail.com from or identifying the email address associated with your ArtistHub account. We may need to verify the request. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and recordkeeping purposes.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

ArtistHub is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use the Service in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the Service. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the situation.

10. Your Choices

You can manage certain profile and content information within the app where those controls are available, adjust notification permissions in your device settings, and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information where applicable.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ArtistHub changes or as legal requirements evolve. We will update the date at the top when the policy changes. Continued use of the Service after an update means you acknowledge the updated policy.
